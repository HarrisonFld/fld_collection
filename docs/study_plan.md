# Study Plan
The general strategy and techniques I've learned

## General Process
1. Reinforce new knowledge after class in a short study session
2. Before the test reaffirm knowledge using flashcards
3. Practice the knowledge before the test using practice quizzes, and reteach anything that was forgotten or left out

## General Tips
* Don't listen to music while studying
* Focus on one goal/topic, don't study aimlessly
* Put the phone away
* Remain in an environment tailored to concentration (library)
